FAQ

Straight answers

Is it really free?

Yes. No card, no catch, no obligation. You get the report either way. If you'd like help with the fixes, we can talk about that after.

Do you need my Google login?

No, not for the audit. We only look at what's public, the same things your customers see. We'd only ask for access if you later want us to make changes for you.

How long does it take?

The form takes about two minutes. We'll confirm when to expect your report when we get your request.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Munster and focus on Cork, Limerick and Kerry. If you're elsewhere in Ireland, send in a request anyway and we'll let you know.

Will you guarantee I get into the top 3?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. Google decides who shows up, and a big part of that is how close the business is to the person searching and how strong the local competition is. What we can do is fix everything in your control, so you're giving yourself the best chance.

What happens with my details?

We use them to do your audit and talk to you about it. Nothing else. We never sell your data. Read the privacy note.
